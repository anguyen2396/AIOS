# About the Business

## Offer

AI Operating System consulting for medium-size businesses. Help owners integrate AI into existing workflows via a 4-rung ladder:

- **Rung 0** — Consulting hour / AIOS setup ($100–$500). First touch. Help owner stand up their own AIOS.
- **Rung 1** — Paid Audit ($500–$2,500). Map workflows, document automation opportunities, write proposal. Pitch after 1–2 Rung 0 sessions.
- **Rung 2** — Focused Build Project ($2,500–$10,000). Ship one end-to-end workflow with measurable ROI. Pitch after audit identifies a clear painful problem.
- **Rung 3** — Retainer ($3,000–$10,000+/month). Ongoing optimization, expansion, training. Pitch after Rung 2 delivers ROI.

## ICP

Medium-size business owners who want to integrate AI into existing operations but don't know where to start or how to do it without breaking what works.

## Revenue model

Pre-revenue stage. GearInc salary (bank transfer) is current primary income. Trading payouts (TopStep) and consulting income not yet flowing — no formal tracking system in place yet.
