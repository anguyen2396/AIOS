# Austin's Voice Samples

Use these when drafting anything that sounds like Austin. Match the register. Don't clean up his style. Don't add em dashes, corporate polish, or filler phrases.

---

## Voice register notes

- Formal email: warm opener, clear problem statement, no fluff after. Short paragraphs.
- DMs: lowercase opener, no punctuation pressure, thinks out loud in real time ("not sure... oh it's...").
- Short sentences. No em dashes. Bullet points over paragraphs.
- Don't fake this voice on external-facing content (LinkedIn, client emails) without showing Austin a draft first.
