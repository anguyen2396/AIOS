# Rung 0 Funnel — Consulting Hour / AIOS Setup

**Price:** $50–$99 for first reps → $100–$500 as you scale
**Goal:** help owner stand up their own AIOS, collect payment, plant Rung 1 seed
**Sources:** warm network + LSV owner introductions

---

## Stage 1 — First Touch (intro message)

Sent via warm intro or directly to someone you know. Keep it short. No jargon.

**Template:**
> Hey [Name], I'm working with a few business owners right now helping them set up a personal AI system — basically an AI that knows their business, their workflow, and can handle research and drafting while they're not at their desk. I've been doing this for [context: myself / through LSV]. Worth a 20-minute call to see if it'd be useful for you?

**Goal of this message:** get the call. Not explain everything.

---

## Stage 2 — Qualification (on the call, first 5 min)

Three questions. If two out of three are yes, proceed.

1. Do you have tasks you do every week that feel repetitive or time-consuming?
2. Do you use email / docs / any kind of note-taking regularly?
3. Are you open to trying something new if it saves you real time?

If they say no to all three: not a fit now. Stay warm, move on.

---

## Stage 3 — The Demo (15–20 min)

Show, don't tell. Use your own AIOS (this repo) as the live demo.

**Demo flow:**
1. Open Claude Code in your AIOS folder
2. Ask: *"What should I focus on this week?"* — show it pulling from your real context files
3. Ask it to draft something (a short email, a summary) — show the voice matching
4. Ask: *"What's on my calendar tomorrow?"* — if connected, show live data; if not, explain that's Day 2
5. Say: "This is what I'd set up for you — but tuned to your business, your workflow, your words"

**Key line at end of demo:**
> "The setup takes about an hour. I walk you through it, we fill in your business context together, and by the end it knows who you are and what matters to you this quarter. That's what I charge [price] for."

---

## Stage 4 — Setup Session (45–60 min, paid)

This is the Rung 0 deliverable. Run it like `/onboard` but for them.

**What you do:**
1. Open a fresh Claude Code session in their AIOS folder (cloned from this repo)
2. Walk through the 7-question intake together — you ask, they answer, you type
3. Handle Q2 (voice samples) strictly — make them paste raw writing, don't let them narrate
4. After intake: scaffold their Day-1 file set live on screen while they watch
5. Show them the closing prompt: *"What should I focus on this week?"* — let them run it themselves

**What they leave with:**
- A working AIOS on their machine with real context
- `context/`, `references/voice.md`, `connections.md`, filled `CLAUDE.md`
- One next step: wire one tool from `connections.md` tomorrow

---

## Stage 5 — Payment

Collect before or at end of session. Don't invoice and wait.

- **For first reps:** $50–$99. Venmo / bank transfer / whatever is frictionless for them.
- **Once you have 2–3 sessions done:** move to $100–$200 minimum.
- **Target:** $300–$500 once you have a case study or visible result to point to.

---

## Stage 6 — Rung 1 Seed (last 5 min of session)

Don't pitch Rung 1 hard. Plant the seed and let it land.

**Script:**
> "Now that I've seen how your business runs — [one specific thing you noticed]. There are probably 2–3 workflows here that could be automated end-to-end, not just AI-assisted. I do a deeper audit for that — map everything out, find the real opportunities, write up a plan. That's a separate engagement. But let's let this sit for a week first and see what you actually use."

**Then stop.** Don't upsell on the same day. Let the AIOS do the work for 7 days. They'll come back asking what else.
